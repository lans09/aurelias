TRANSPARENCY & SECURITY

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to connect my wallet or sign a transaction?

No. AURELIAS never asks for wallet connections, signature requests, or token approvals. You only paste your sending address and transfer $2 directly from your own wallet app, eliminating drainer risk.

How are the 36 winners selected and verified?

When the 72-hour Season 04 round closes, Chainlink VRF v2.5 generates cryptographically verifiable randomness to draw 1 Grand Prize winner (20,000 USDC), 5 Runner-Up winners (3,000 USDC each), and 30 Community winners (500 USDC each). Winning addresses and payout transaction hashes are published publicly.

Which assets can I use to pay the $2 entry fee?

You can enter using ETH, BNB, USDT, TON, Solana, or Bitcoin. Select your preferred asset in the entry card, submit your sending address, and send the exact $2 equivalent within the 15-minute window.

How do I know my $2 payment is confirmed?

Keep the QR payment screen open after sending. As soon as your transfer is verified, your card automatically updates to PAYMENT RECEIVED · IN DRAW and displays your Ticket ID.

How do winners receive their USDC payouts?

Payouts are sent directly on-chain to the address you entered with. If you included a Telegram handle, you also receive direct confirmation once your prize transaction is broadcast.
